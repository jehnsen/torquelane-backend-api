Request pipeline

Every write takes the same path through the API. Pick a request, press Play, and see which layer answers it. A refusal inside the transaction leaves nothing behind.

    Rule R3. Controllers hold no business rules. The Action owns one DB::transaction and locks the rows it reads. Events and jobs are dispatched only after the commit.

      Maintenance engine

      A service item falls due on whichever limit arrives first. The engine turns the distance limit into a date using the vehicle's average daily use, so both limits can be compared on one calendar.

      Stock ledger

      The shop's stock room is an append-only list of moves. The balance and its moving average cost are worked out from them, and every move posts its own journal entry in the same transaction.

      Balance

      stock_moves (append-only)

      Journal entries posted

      Rules shown. Inbound goods blend into the average, rounded half up to a centavo. Outbound goods leave at the average, which does not change. The Inventory line is the change in book value, so any rounding difference is posted as its own visible line.

      Invoice to books

      An invoice for ₱6,830.00 of parts and ₱1,820.00 of labour. Issue it, take payments, and watch the accounts move. Money received is a customer deposit first, and each allocation moves it to the receivable.

      Accounts

      Journal

      Rules shown. VAT is rounded once per total, never per line. An issued invoice is frozen and can only be voided, and only when nothing is paid against it. paid_cents is re-summed from the standing allocations, never incremented. A work order's collected_at is stamped when its invoice becomes paid.

      Detailing booking

      A booking is a budget of 30-minute slots that fills working days one after another. The branch has a pool of detailers, and each booking reserves heads on every slot it touches. Click a slot to block or unblock it.

      Rules shown. The first day starts at the nearest slot, with ties going forward. Closing only caps where a day's work may start, and the rest rolls to the next morning. The tightest slot must have the package's minimum free, and the reservation is trimmed to what is free.

      app/Domain/Detailing/{SlotGrid,DayPlanner,BookingCapacity,ServiceDuration}.php · app/Actions/Detailing/TakeBooking.php
